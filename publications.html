Agriculture

Tephrosia for Livestock Parasites: Farmer Practices

FGDsQualitativeAuthor

Six focus group discussions with 58 farmers across five Migori sub-counties (13 to 17 January 2025), following up the December 2024 farmer survey for the Rural Farmers Research Network. Farmers found tephrosia effective against livestock parasites, best used as prevention; ratio 1:5 worked best for most, while 1:3 was risky for young animals. Prepared by Michael Chariga.

Fermented Feeds Lab Results: CoP Presentation

PresentationNutrient analysisFarmer Research Network

Lab nutrient results for fermented poultry feeds from five organisations (CRANE, MHAC, RFRN, RODI, SOFDI), on a dry matter basis and checked against optimal ranges. Crude protein and digestible energy were low for most farmers; calcium and fibre need review in RFRN and SOFDI. Presented at the 2026 Kenya Agroecology Hub Community of Practice meeting, Kitale.

Student Learning and a Farmer Research Network

Pilot studyEducationCo-author

Six-month pilot (two school terms) with 171 fifth-grade pupils in Kurutiyange and Naora, Kuria West, testing whether pupils could help a farmer research network collect data while building practical, CBC-aligned skills. The hypothesis that pupils would collect better data than their parents was rejected, but the learning gains were clear. Co-authored with Tekviah Munandi, Esther Njeri and Evans Odhiambo.
